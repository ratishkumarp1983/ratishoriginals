1. Executive Summary
Scriptory is a digital publishing platform that enables an author to publish, protect, market, and monetize written works through an online reading experience.
Initially, the platform will host only the owner's original writings.
Future architecture must support:
Multiple authors
Revenue sharing
Author onboarding
Marketplace capabilities
The platform will provide:
Secure document publishing
Sample reading
Digital purchases
Membership subscriptions
Coupon management
Protected reading experience
Reader analytics
Razorpay payment integration
Google SSO authentication
The platform is not intended to be a file-sharing website.
It is intended to be a premium digital reading platform.

FR-12 Verified Reviews & Ratings
The platform shall allow authenticated users to rate and review books that they have legitimately accessed.
## Eligibility Rules
A user may submit a review only if at least one of the following conditions is true:
- The user has purchased the document.
- The document is included in the user's active membership.
- The administrator has explicitly granted access.
Visitors and users without access shall not be allowed to submit ratings or reviews.
## Review Features
Each review shall contain:
- Rating (1–5 stars)
- Review Title (optional)
- Review Text
- Date Submitted
- Verified Purchase Badge
- Verified Member Badge (if applicable)
## Review Editing
Users may:
- Edit their review.
- Update their rating.
- Delete their review.
The system shall maintain an audit trail of review edits.
## Review Moderation
The administrator shall be able to:
- Hide reviews.
- Delete reviews.
- Pin featured reviews.
- Reply publicly to reviews.
- Report abusive reviews.
- Restore hidden reviews.
## Review Display
Each book page shall display:
- Average Rating
- Total Number of Ratings
- Rating Distribution
- Featured Reviews
- Latest Reviews
Example:
★★★★☆ (4.7)
Based on 324 verified readers
★★★★★ 210
★★★★ 82
★★★ 21
★★ 7
★ 4
## Review Sorting
Readers may sort reviews by:
- Most Helpful
- Highest Rated
- Lowest Rated
- Most Recent
- Oldest
## Helpful Votes
Readers may mark reviews as:
Helpful
or
Not Helpful
The number of Helpful votes shall be displayed.
## Spoiler Protection
When submitting a review, users may mark it as containing spoilers.
Spoiler reviews shall remain collapsed until expanded by the reader.
## Review Analytics
The administrator dashboard shall display:
- Average Rating
- Review Count
- Rating Trends
- Most Helpful Reviews
- Books with Highest Ratings
- Books with Lowest Ratings
## Future AI Features
The platform architecture shall support future AI-powered review features including:
- AI-generated review summaries
- Sentiment analysis
- Spam detection
- Toxic language detection
- Fake review detection
- Review topic extraction
